# Reading-view concept image prompts

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Each image used the shared prompt followed by its individual concept prompt. No reference images were supplied. Background transparency was disabled.

## Shared prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE rough concept image for an iPad-first reading view for an existing Obsidian notes vault. This is an early design exploration, not a finished product or marketing image. Flat, front-facing landscape iPad screen with a very thin device outline, no perspective, no desk, hands, props, or photography. A clean legible low-to-medium fidelity product sketch: warm paper, graphite-gray text and lines, restrained muted sage-green for current and bookmarked positions. Simple squared paper surfaces and a little pencil-like softness, generous readable typography. Show the interface large, nearly filling the image. Put the concept name as a clear small caption above the screen. Make the interaction model unmistakable.
Shared product: folders are section dividers, notes ordered automatically, short and long notes mixed, no manual organization. Compact top bar "Fieldnotes" with controls "A–Z / Date" and "Folders". Folder names "Ideas", "Reading", "Walks"; Journal excluded via settings. Current note title "Attention is a material", with brief real text "What we notice shapes what we can make." and a few short paragraph lines. Held note "Gardens and paths". Use short text and schematic paragraph lines instead of dense illegible writing. Bottom controls "Hold place", "Swap", "Compare". Two persistent positions must be visually marked CURRENT and HELD where appropriate. No invented AI chat, statistics, productivity dashboard, editing toolbar, or graph network. Focus on the requested distinct layout.

## two-place-desk.png

LEFT page visibly labeled "HELD · A" with bookmark ribbon, title "Gardens and paths", brief text "A garden is organized, but it keeps growing beyond the organization." RIGHT page labeled "EXPLORING · B", title "Attention is a material", text "What we notice shapes what we can make." Both pages contain a few believable paragraph lines. ## wandering-notebook.png

Concept name: "Wandering notebook". One large reading page in center with visible linked phrases or a small group "Links in this note" near bottom including "Constraints make room" and "Courtyard observations". Above the page, a SHORT READING TRAIL of linked note titles joined by thin arrows, not a complex graph: "Gardens and paths → Slow knowledge → Attention is a material". At the first trail title show a ribbon and label "Held origin"; current trail title has a sage underline. A visible small side bookmark tab or slim margin slip says "Return to Gardens and paths", visually separated from the trail so the origin survives further browsing. Below page have the automatic folder sequence navigation "← Previous" and "Next →" as well as the shared controls. An understated annotation near the trail says "Follow a thought; keep your place". Feel like exploratory reading with a clear way home, not browsing history in a web browser. No wide list sidebar, no contact sheet, no network visualization.
